01What types of vintage wedding cars can I book?+

Availability may include open-top vintage cars, enclosed classics and royal-style wedding cars. Share your date and city to receive the current fleet options.

02Is a chauffeur included with the wedding car?+

ROYALS offers chauffeur-driven bookings. The exact reporting time, route, waiting period and inclusions are confirmed in your quotation.

03How much does a vintage wedding car rental cost?+

Pricing depends on the selected car, wedding date, city, rental duration, delivery distance and decoration requirements. Request a personalised quotation for accurate pricing.

04How early should I reserve my wedding car?+

Book as early as possible for peak wedding dates. Early enquiries provide the best chance of securing your preferred car and coordinating venue logistics.

05Can the car be decorated to match our wedding theme?+

Decoration options can be discussed during booking. Share reference colours or floral preferences so suitable arrangements and any additional charges can be confirmed.

06Do you deliver wedding cars outside Vadodara?+

Yes, delivery may be arranged across Gujarat, including Ahmedabad, Surat, Gandhinagar, Anand, Nadiad and Bharuch, subject to availability and transport planning.

07Can I rent a vintage car for a pre-wedding shoot?+

Yes. Vintage cars can be booked for pre-wedding photography, engagement films, reels and selected creative productions.

08What details should I send for an availability check?+

Send your name, phone number, event date, city, venue, occasion, preferred car style and approximate reporting time.
